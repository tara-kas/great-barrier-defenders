# Asset provenance

The textures in `materials/seabed/` are downloaded, unmodified 2K JPEG PBR maps from Poly Haven. They are distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as confirmed by [Poly Haven's asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Credit is included for provenance even though CC0 does not require attribution.

| Local prefix | Source asset | Artist | Native physical width | Maps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fine_sand_` | [Sand 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_03) | Charlotte Baglioni | 2 m | diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness |
| `rubble_` | [Coral Ground 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/coral_ground_02) | Rob Tuytel | 2 m | diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness |
| `sand_` | [Aerial Beach 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_beach_01) | Rob Tuytel | 30 m | diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness |

The actual downloaded URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `materials/manifest.json`. All textures are bundled locally; no network request is required to render them. No Poly Haven logo or third-party preview render is included.

The coral maps in `materials/coral/` are original, deterministic procedural assets generated by `scripts/build_material_assets.py`. They are simulation appearance approximations of tiny calice cups, rims and tissue grain, not scans or claims about a specific biological species. They are provided with the project; the generator is the authoritative reproducible source.

`corals/` contains a Smithsonian Open Access CC0 1.0 scan of *Acropora secale*,
USNM 268, credited to the Smithsonian Institution, National Museum of Natural
History, and The Hydrous coral digitization project. The derived geometry has
been trimmed, decimated, normalized and recoloured for the scenario. The
original source is retained. See [scan provenance](../docs/SCANNED_CORALS.md)
and `corals/manifest.json` for official records, licensing and checksums.

`pocillopora/` contains the Smithsonian Open Access scan of
*Pocillopora damicornis*, USNM 46926, credited to the Smithsonian Institution,
National Museum of Natural History, and The Hydrous coral digitization project.
The [official record](https://3d.si.edu/es/object/3d/pocillopora-damicornis%3Aae26ad01-e7be-4664-8a3a-b8bffd42a186)
identifies its 3D media as public domain; metadata is CC0. The original GLB,
source preview and extracted textures are retained. Derived geometry is welded,
decimated and normalized without basal trimming; scenario recolouring is not
part of the original specimen. See [Pocillopora provenance](../docs/POCILLOPORA_SCAN.md)
and `pocillopora/manifest.json` for licensing, conversion and checksums.
The Smithsonian does not endorse this simulation.
