# Simulation variables and assumptions

The deterministic mission engine owns vehicle poses, manipulation phases and persistent fragment records. Isaac Sim 6.0 renders those states. `configs/natural_reef.json` selects the current natural-branch contact, detailed tissue, moving fans/polyps and visible inspection-light pilot. The review workflow records files on Spark and copies completed MP4s locally; it creates no streaming server. Recording-specific completion evidence is kept in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).

## Implemented variables

| Variable | Configuration or state | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layout seed | `seed` | Reproducible mission colonies, scenery and observation errors |
| Habitat density | `render.reef_density` | Clamped 0.5–1.5; default 580 decorative colonies in connected patches |
| Fish population | `render.fish_count` | Clamped 0–72; default 42 fish in three deterministic schools |
| Reef size/depth | `world` | Vehicle coordinate bounds and scene dimensions |
| Region health/policy | `regions` | Living donors, monitoring, bleached-reef assistance mounting and unsafe rubble |
| Visibility, metres | `water.visibility` | Observation range, adaptive survey altitude and image attenuation |
| Turbidity, 0–1 | `water.turbidity` | Observation confidence and renderer fog |
| Current, m/s | `water.current` | Vehicle drag; mean flow driving bounded soft-fan and polyp motion with an illustrative oscillatory surge |
| Soft coral detail | `render.soft_coral_count`, `soft_coral_height`, `soft_coral_radius`, `moving_polyp_count` | Clear-pocket fan placement and living polyp patches; `natural_reef.json` requests 12 fans and up to 24 polyp anchors per living colony |
| Tissue/scan detail | `render.realistic_tissue` | Full source-resolution hero scans and mapped tissue materials; background scans retain the reduced mesh |
| Mass/displacement/density | `robot.mass`, `robot.displaced_volume`, `water.density` | Vehicle weight, buoyancy and acceleration |
| Speed and thrust | `robot.max_speed`, `robot.max_thrust` | Bounded vehicle control |
| Drag | `robot.linear_drag`, `robot.quadratic_drag` | Per-axis resistance relative to the water |
| Camera cone/period | `robot.camera_half_fov_degrees`, `robot.camera_period` | Visibility-limited observations |
| Donor cutting capacity | `donors.fragments_per_colony`, `donors.max_fragments` | Finite pre-existing branch cuttings on healthy donors |
| Manipulation durations | `mission.approach_duration`, `close_duration`, `detach_duration`, `retract_duration`, `lower_duration`, `secure_duration`, `open_duration`, `retreat_duration` | Explicit and reviewable grasp, carry and release sequence |
| Attachment method | `mission.attachment_method` | `same_genet_contact` in `configs/natural_reef.json`; `limestone_outplant` in the baseline |
| Contact pose | Coral/observation `planting_point`, `contact_fixture` | Candidate triangle on the actual recipient scan, contact normal and small basal securing-compound anchor |
| Compatibility | Coral `genet_id`, `genotype_known`, species | Known same-genet and same-species donor required in contact mode |
| Inspection lighting | `inspection.mode`, `ambient_scale`, `range_m`, `half_angle_degrees`, `attenuation`, `emission_gain` | Wrist white/blue/UV modes with an illustrative visible host-fluorescence response |
| Visible aiming light | `inspection.visible_uv_pilot`, `uv_pilot_intensity`, `render.volumetric_water` | Co-aligned visible violet light and renderer water scattering make the inspection footprint visible; UV itself stays invisible |
| Gripper state | `gripper_world`, `gripper_open`, `phase_progress` | Authoritative jaw position and visible opening/closing |
| Fragment identity/pose | `fragments[*]` | Same cut branch moves continuously from donor to mount |
| Robot availability | API `set_robot_available` | Temporary hold/resume retaining the current fragment and reservation |

## Explicit assumptions

- **Reef setting:** a Great Barrier Reef-inspired artistic mosaic of living, bleached and dead patches, not a georeferenced digital twin. Registered mission colonies are the 24 surveyed targets. Hundreds of extra low-profile scenery specimens create visual cover without becoming hidden mission targets or tall navigation obstacles.
- **Dynamics:** reduced 3D translation with buoyancy, current-relative drag and bounded thrust. Yaw follows travel direction. There is no CFD, added mass, full rotational hydrodynamics or propeller-wake simulation. Isaac renders externally integrated poses rather than solving vehicle dynamics a second time in PhysX.
- **Perception:** an analytical downward visibility cone, rock occlusion, seeded position error and synthetic morphology/health labels. Exact localization and annotated close-work poses are assumed. This is not a trained coral detector, underwater SLAM or stereo reconstruction pipeline. Planning uses observed eligible colonies and donors; world truth supports rendering and evaluation.
- **Optics:** renderer water scattering/fog, lighting, particles and approximate channel-dependent attenuation/backscatter. Ideal renderer depth drives optical processing. Wrist white, blue (450 nm) and UV (365 nm) modes use an illustrative RGB fluorescence response. Detailed scanned colonies gate emission per vertex by cone, range, attenuation and surface facing, with conservative colony/rock occlusion. This is not spectral radiative transfer or exact self-shadowing of excitation. UV itself is invisible; the optional co-aligned violet pilot is a separate visible channel whose cone/footprint can scatter in the water. Source-off and nonfluorescent controls have zero host emission; dead controls exclude host signal as a scenario assumption, not a claim that real dead skeletons or algae cannot fluoresce. Water optics, fluorescence and camera housings are not calibrated. Colour and signal brightness do not identify genotype or prove health.
- **Cameras:** each robot PiP is an independent rendered camera synchronized to the director's scene state. The worker view comes from a camera pod on a bracket attached to its gripper. The spectator image uses 1.7× configured visibility for legibility; robot feeds use configured visibility directly. None of these rendered pixels supplies the current synthetic perception labels.
- **Manipulation:** jaws visibly close on the stem before detachment; the held branch follows the jaw centre; it is seated before the jaws open; the empty gripper then withdraws. An analytical visual arm follows the authoritative jaw target. Force closure, soft-tissue deformation, cutter mechanics, adhesive curing and floating-base reaction forces remain abstracted.
- **Fragment continuity:** one small rootless cutting exists on a living donor from scene creation and keeps its ID, dimensions, seed and shape after transfer. Its cut base moves through donor attachment, grasping, carrying and placement. No new coral/roots mesh is generated at planting, and there is no scale or growth animation.
- **Contact restoration:** `configs/natural_reef.json` selects `same_genet_contact`. Only observed healthy donors with a known matching genet and species can supply cuttings; unknown or incompatible lineages hold without harvesting. The lineage registry is synthetic scenario ground truth. A candidate is selected on an accessible triangle of the existing recipient scan; no recipient extension branch is fabricated. Only a small basal securing compound is separate geometry. The renderer audits transformed cutting vertices against the actual recipient triangles and checks that the selected triangle still matches the unchanged scan. This is not a contact-force, tissue-fusion or adhesive simulation. The intended recipient contact patch is the owner-clearance exception; other coral/rock workspaces retain 0.55 m clearance and contact sites stay at least 0.70 m apart.
- **Historical outplant baseline:** `configs/cinematic.json` retains `limestone_outplant`: a healthy cutting is secured to a separate limestone patch beside a bleached colony. Every colony/rock footprint retains 0.55 m workspace clearance. This baseline does not represent the tissue-contact treatment.
- **Biology:** only bleached but living colonies receive assistance; dead colonies remain excluded even if a region permits planting. The contact scenario follows the principle of the *Pocillopora damicornis* same-genet study, but its enlarged 14 cm cutting, in-situ recipient fixture and robot workflow are engineering proxies, not a validated field protocol. The recipient stays `bleached_alive`; tissue fusion, symbiont transfer, growth and recovery over days/weeks are not simulated. Sustainable harvesting, disease, survival and ecological benefit remain outside scope. Temperature, salinity, pH and nutrients are fixed. See [primary study and implementation boundaries](INSPECTION_AND_CONTACT.md).
- **Coral appearance:** the Smithsonian *P. damicornis* asset supplies real skeletal morphology from a dried museum specimen, not either experimental lineage. Registered living/bleached hero colonies preserve all 150,000 source triangles and original UV seams; selected background colonies use the 19,908-triangle reduction. Mapped diffuse/roughness/normal detail replaces uniform tinting. Partial-bleaching maps retain warm ivory tissue, residual tan/pink pigment islands and calice contrast; these are artistic living-tissue cues, not a measured bleaching-severity model. Dead procedural controls remain structurally distinct and receive no living polyp patch.
- **Coral motion:** hard skeletons and manipulated cuttings remain rigid. Soft fan vertices and living polyp tentacles deform under mean current plus a 6.5-second illustrative surge/transverse-flow cycle. A damped drag response, anchored roots/calice cups and deformation limits control the motion; normals update with the geometry. Absolute-time evaluation supports reproducible frame skipping and a zero-current control remains still. The polyps are enlarged for the demo, and this reduced model is not CFD or calibrated fluid–structure interaction. Pitted limestone geometry and scanned seabed materials remain visual habitat detail.
- **Fauna:** fish are visual actors with deterministic schooling, tail motion, reef/vehicle avoidance and worksite exclusions. They do not supply mission observations and are not learned agents or validated animal behaviour models.
- **Coordination:** two role-specialized robots share a centralized map and task registry. Availability means a temporary hold; permanent-failure handover and acoustic communications are not implemented.
- **Safety:** path planning and separation guards reject unsafe motion. A zero collision count records this guard behaviour, not validated contact forces.

## Scope of validation

Tests cover complete missions, finite donor stock, donor/target health, same-genet compatibility and mismatch holds, actual scan-surface contact, inspection negative controls, bounded fan/polyp motion, reservation uniqueness, persistent fragment identity, continuous position/yaw across handoffs, jaw closure before detachment, carrying pose, opening before release and fixed placement during retreat. The detail recorder checks fixed cameras, zero-current controls and tracked fan-tip movement; hard-skeleton hashes and mesh topology stay fixed while flagged soft-tissue points may move. Renderer/camera evidence and the final movie's validation status belong in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). A successful engineering transfer does not establish real-world coral restoration efficacy.
