ABOUT THIS VIEW

Explore a living mission.

This WebGL scene is a portable preview of the mission running in the Python backend. The Isaac Sim 6.0 adapter renders the same world separately on DGX Spark.

Coral geometry, water effects and vehicle models are procedural. Perception and hydrodynamics are simplified models. Planting attaches a fragment; it does not instantly revive dead coral.

Use the visibility and current controls to change actual mission conditions.
