MODEL & PROVENANCE

A reef you can interrogate.

This lab uses Three.js, scanned Smithsonian coral specimens, textured reef substrate, and a deterministic browser simulation. Coral geometry uses identified Smithsonian specimens, with living tissue colours reconstructed artistically. The inclined reef is a habitat composition, not a surveyed reconstruction. Fish and crabs are animated geometric proxies. Coral skeletons and rocks stay rigid; tiny living-polyp tentacles have bounded, current-driven visual motion, not calibrated elastic physics. The irregular healthy and bleached regions are seeded scenario compositions with mixed transitions.

Vehicle motion uses reduced thrust, buoyancy and drag, terrain clearance and marine-life avoidance. Sonar returns are analytical range observations; sound propagation and marine animal behavior are not calibrated. Dropped fragments, cutting quality, alignment checks and recovery limits are explicit engineering models. This is not an Isaac Sim contact solver, calibrated cutter mechanics, or proof that coral tissue will recover.

During the mission, Gemma perception feeds an inspectable policy and cuOpt route optimization. Argon may advise rescan, hold or abort during uncertain or failed stages while the robot is held. At most three requests are allowed per episode; advice cannot authorize handling or bypass fresh Gemma evidence. It also reviews completed logs for human consideration. Configured adapters receive actual rendered tool-camera frames and mission telemetry after model startup and mission start. Telemetry connects automatically on page load; the mission remains paused until you start it. Runtime placement labels distinguish robot, base-station and optional Modal hosting; configuration alone does not establish a successful model run. Missing or warming services keep autonomous operation paused. Uncertain or unsafe model observations hold for fresh evidence; retry, rescan or abort without bypassing the perception gates. No remote controller can override health, lineage or placement checks.
