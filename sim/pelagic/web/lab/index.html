MODEL & PROVENANCE

A reef you can interrogate.

This lab uses Three.js, scanned Smithsonian coral specimens, textured reef substrate, and a deterministic browser simulation. Coral geometry uses identified Smithsonian specimens, with living tissue colours reconstructed artistically. The inclined reef is a habitat composition, not a surveyed reconstruction. Fish and crabs are animated geometric proxies.

Vehicle motion uses reduced thrust, buoyancy and drag, terrain clearance and marine-life avoidance. Sonar returns are analytical range observations; sound propagation and marine animal behavior are not calibrated. Dropped fragments, cutting quality, alignment checks and recovery limits are explicit engineering models. This is not an Isaac Sim contact solver, calibrated cutter mechanics, or proof that coral tissue will recover.

Gemini makes bounded high-level decisions from a rendered tool-camera image and sonar telemetry when a server credential is configured. Selecting Gemini sends those simulation inputs to Google at decision gates. Scripted control works offline. Neither controller can override health, lineage or placement checks. API failures hold the mission visibly.
